# turn-usage privacy policy

turn-usage runs entirely inside Claude Code on your computer. It does not collect, send or share any data with its author or anyone else, and it makes no network requests.

## What it reads

- **Your usage figures**, from Claude Code: the percentage used of your five-hour and weekly usage windows, when they reset, and the estimated cost of the session. These are the same figures Claude Code's status line and `/cost` show.
- **Claude Code's `language` setting**, to show the line in English or Spanish.
- **The final text of each Claude reply**, to tell which block of the reply the line goes under.

## What it keeps

For the current session only, it keeps the final text of up to 100 recent replies, each with its usage line, in Claude Code's session memory. Nothing is written to disk by the plugin, and it keeps nothing from one session to the next.

## What it sends

Nothing. turn-usage has no servers, no analytics and no third parties, and it does not read credentials, run commands or touch any files.
